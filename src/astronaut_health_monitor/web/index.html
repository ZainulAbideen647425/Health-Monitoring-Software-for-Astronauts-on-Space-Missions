Astronaut Health Monitor

Check your health during the mission. This is a prototype, not a medical device.

Welcome

This tool helps you keep an eye on your health far from Earth.

  1. Add your readings (heart rate, sleep, mood and more).
  2. See in plain words what they mean.
  3. Know exactly when to ask the flight surgeon for help.

What to do now

    Your readings

    ✓ Good   ▲ Watch   ■ Urgent. Worst first. Tap "Why, and what to do" on any card.

    Your body

    Hover or tap a part for a quick reading. Tap it again (or press Enter) for the full context. Keys 1 to 5 pick a part, Esc closes, ? opens help.

    How it changed

    The green band is the healthy range. Amber dots are outside it.
